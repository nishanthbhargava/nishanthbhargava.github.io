AI-Powered Learning Case Study

Project Metadata

  • Course: Entrepreneurial Finance
  • Unit: Unit 1 – Overview of Entrepreneurial Finance
  • Video Title: What is Entrepreneurial Finance?
  • Duration: 7–8 Minutes
  • BTL Level: BTL 1 (Remember)

Learning Objectives

  1. Define entrepreneurial finance and recall its scope within business finance.
  2. Identify financial challenges faced by startups.
  3. List key differences between entrepreneurial and corporate finance.
  4. Recognise its importance in India’s startup ecosystem.

Instructional Design Approach

This video was structured using a clear pedagogical flow:

  • Concept definition
  • Comparative analysis (Entrepreneurial vs Corporate Finance)
  • Contextual challenges (India-focused data)
  • Applied case study (NutriBasket example)
  • Summary and reinforcement

The script was drafted using ChatGPT as an ideation assistant, then refined, structured, and aligned to learning objectives manually. The final AI avatar video was generated using HeyGen and edited in Adobe Premiere Pro.

Selected Script Excerpt

Opening:

Welcome to the Entrepreneurial Finance course. Over the coming videos, we shall explore how entrepreneurs make critical financial decisions, how startups raise and manage capital, and how the financial ecosystem supports innovation and new venture creation.

Entrepreneurial finance is the study and practice of how new ventures make financial decisions, raise capital, manage resources, and create value under conditions of extreme uncertainty.

Unlike traditional corporate finance, startups operate under deep uncertainty, severe information asymmetry, constrained access to capital, and persistent early-stage cash burn.
